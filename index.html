About Me

Introduce

I'm a Biomaterials Engineering student at Alfred University (minor in Communication Studies), currently running exploratory studies on Co-doped glasses in Dr. Möncke's postgrad research group — using UV-Vis spectroscopy to read how transition metals sit inside a glass network.

Outside the lab, I co-founded Grace Reaches On Wheels, a public-health nonprofit tackling non-communicable disease prevention in rural Tanzania, and SERA Access, a platform centralizing university applications for students across East Africa. I also founded and host The Icons Digital Hub Africa, a podcast built to put young global voices on record.
